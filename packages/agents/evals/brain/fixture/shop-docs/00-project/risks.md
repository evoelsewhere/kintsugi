# Risks

The risks the program follows, with their owner and what reduces them.

| ID | Risk | Owner | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | Prices differ between the old batch and the new services during coexistence | Delivery lead | Nightly reconciliation of every invoice |
| R-02 | The mainframe team is not available after the switch-off | Program lead | Knowledge transfer sessions recorded before the switch-off |
| R-03 | Customer data exposed in the landing zone | Security officer | Exported files are encrypted at rest and deleted once loaded |
