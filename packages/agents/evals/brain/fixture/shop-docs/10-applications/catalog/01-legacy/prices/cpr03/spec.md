# Store prices

How stores may differ from the national price.

## Business rules

### RULE-CPR03-0001: Local price

A store may sell an article below the national price, never above it.

### RULE-CPR03-0002: Local limit

A local price is at most 15% below the national price.

### RULE-CPR03-0003: Local expiry

A local price ends after 28 days unless renewed.
