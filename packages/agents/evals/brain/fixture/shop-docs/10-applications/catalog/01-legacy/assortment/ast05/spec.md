# Seasonal articles

How articles sold only part of the year are handled.

## Business rules

### RULE-AST05-0001: Season window

A seasonal article is listed from the start to the end date of its season.

### RULE-AST05-0002: Season preview

A seasonal article may be shown 14 days before its season without being sold.

### RULE-AST05-0003: Season stock

Unsold seasonal stock is moved to the outlet when the season ends.
