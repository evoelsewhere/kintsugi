# Price matching

How the shop answers a lower price elsewhere.

## Business rules

### RULE-CPR06-0001: Match request

A customer may ask for a lower price seen at a listed competitor within 7 days.

### RULE-CPR06-0002: Match proof

A match needs a screenshot or a receipt from the competitor.

### RULE-CPR06-0003: Match exclusions

Marketplace sellers are never matched.
