# Article variants

How sizes and colours of one article are grouped.

## Business rules

### RULE-AST03-0001: Variant group

Variants of one article share its number and differ by a two-digit suffix.

### RULE-AST03-0002: Variant limit

An article has at most 40 variants.

### RULE-AST03-0003: Variant prices

Every variant of an article has the same base price.
