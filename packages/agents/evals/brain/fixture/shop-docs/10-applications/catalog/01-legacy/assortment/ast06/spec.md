# Restricted articles

How articles with a legal restriction are sold.

## Business rules

### RULE-AST06-0001: Age check

An article restricted by age is sold only after the customer confirms their age at checkout.

### RULE-AST06-0002: Restricted regions

An article restricted in a region is hidden to customers of that region.

### RULE-AST06-0003: Restricted delivery

A restricted article is never left at a pick-up point.
