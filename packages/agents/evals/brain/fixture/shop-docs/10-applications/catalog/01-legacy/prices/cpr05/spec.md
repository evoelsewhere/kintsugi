# Unit prices

How the price per kilogram or litre is shown.

## Business rules

### RULE-CPR05-0001: Unit price shown

Articles sold by weight or volume show their price per kilogram or per litre.

### RULE-CPR05-0002: Unit price digits

A unit price is shown with two decimals.

### RULE-CPR05-0003: Unit price source

The unit price is computed from the net content on the label.
