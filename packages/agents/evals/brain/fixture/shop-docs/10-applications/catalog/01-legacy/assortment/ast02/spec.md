# Delisting articles

How the batch removes an article from the catalog.

## Business rules

### RULE-AST02-0001: Delisting notice

An article is delisted 30 days after the supplier gives notice.

### RULE-AST02-0002: Remaining stock

Remaining stock of a delisted article is sold at a markdown of 20%.

### RULE-AST02-0003: Delisting history

A delisted article keeps its page, marked as no longer sold, for one year.
