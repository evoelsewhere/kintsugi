# Base prices

How the base price of an article is set.

## Business rules

### RULE-CPR01-0001: Price source

The base price comes from the supplier price list plus the category margin.

### RULE-CPR01-0002: Price review

Base prices are reviewed every quarter.

### RULE-CPR01-0003: Price floor

A base price is never below the purchase price.
