# Bundles

How articles sold together are listed.

## Business rules

### RULE-AST04-0001: Bundle content

A bundle holds two to five articles that are also sold alone.

### RULE-AST04-0002: Bundle stock

A bundle is in stock when every article in it is.

### RULE-AST04-0003: Bundle listing

A bundle is listed under the category of its most expensive article.
