# Listing new articles

How the batch lists a new article in the catalog.

## Business rules

### RULE-AST01-0001: Listing approval

A new article is listed only after the category manager approves its sheet.

### RULE-AST01-0002: Listing date

A listed article becomes visible on the first Monday after its approval.

### RULE-AST01-0003: Listing photos

An article without at least two photos stays hidden.
