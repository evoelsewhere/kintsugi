# Price changes

How a change of price reaches the shop.

## Business rules

### RULE-CPR02-0001: Change timing

A price change takes effect at midnight on its start date.

### RULE-CPR02-0002: Change notice

A rise of more than 10% is announced to the stores a week ahead.

### RULE-CPR02-0003: Change history

Every price change is kept with who made it.
