# Backorder expiry

What happens to a backorder that waits too long.

## Business rules

### RULE-BKO02-0001: Expiry period

A backorder line still undelivered 60 days after the order is dropped by the batch.

### RULE-BKO02-0002: Expiry notice

The customer is told by e-mail when a line is dropped.

### RULE-BKO02-0003: Expiry payment

A dropped line that was paid is refunded the same day.
