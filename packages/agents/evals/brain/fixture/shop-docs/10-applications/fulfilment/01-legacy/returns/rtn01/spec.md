# Unopened articles

How an unopened article is taken back.

## Business rules

### RULE-RTN01-0001: Unopened window

An unopened article may be sent back within 30 days of delivery.

### RULE-RTN01-0002: Unopened refund

The full price is refunded once the article is checked.

### RULE-RTN01-0003: Unopened shipping

Return shipping of an unopened article is free.
