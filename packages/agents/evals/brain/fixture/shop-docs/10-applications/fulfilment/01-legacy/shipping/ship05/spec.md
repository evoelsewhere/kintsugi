# Pick-up points

How parcels are left at pick-up points.

## Business rules

### RULE-SHP05-0001: Pick-up choice

A customer may choose any pick-up point within 20 km of their address.

### RULE-SHP05-0002: Pick-up holding

A parcel waits 10 days at the pick-up point, then goes back.

### RULE-SHP05-0003: Pick-up size

A parcel larger than 60 cm on any side is never sent to a pick-up point.
