# Substitutes

How a substitute is offered for a backorder.

## Business rules

### RULE-BKO06-0001: Substitute offer

The buyers may offer a substitute of the same or a higher price.

### RULE-BKO06-0002: Substitute price

The customer pays the price of the article ordered.

### RULE-BKO06-0003: Substitute answer

Without an answer within 7 days the backorder goes on.
