# Personalised articles

How personalised articles are treated.

## Business rules

### RULE-RTN05-0001: Personalised window

A personalised article is never taken back unless it is faulty.

### RULE-RTN05-0002: Personalised fault

A faulty personalised article is made again.

### RULE-RTN05-0003: Personalised notice

The checkout says so before a personalised article is paid.
