# Freight

How bulky consignments are sent.

## Business rules

### RULE-SHP03-0001: Freight carrier

A consignment above that weight travels with the haulier Northline, booked a day ahead.

### RULE-SHP03-0002: Freight slot

The customer chooses a four-hour delivery slot for a freight consignment.

### RULE-SHP03-0003: Freight unloading

The haulier unloads at the kerb only.
