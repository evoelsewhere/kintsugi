# Standard parcels

How ordinary parcels are sent.

## Business rules

### RULE-SHP02-0001: Standard carrier

A parcel of up to 30 kg goes with the parcel carrier Parcelway.

### RULE-SHP02-0002: Standard tracking

Every standard parcel gets a tracking number sent by e-mail.

### RULE-SHP02-0003: Standard attempts

Parcelway tries to deliver twice, then leaves the parcel at a pick-up point.
