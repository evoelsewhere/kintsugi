# Backorder checks

How the batch follows backorders.

## Business rules

### RULE-BKO04-0001: Nightly check

Every backorder line is checked against incoming stock every night.

### RULE-BKO04-0002: Supplier chase

A supplier is chased when its date has passed by three days.

### RULE-BKO04-0003: Check report

The buyers receive a weekly list of late backorder lines.
