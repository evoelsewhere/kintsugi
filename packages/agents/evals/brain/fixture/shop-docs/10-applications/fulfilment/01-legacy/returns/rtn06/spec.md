# Refund payment

How a refund reaches the customer.

## Business rules

### RULE-RTN06-0001: Refund method

A refund goes back to the means of payment used for the order.

### RULE-RTN06-0002: Refund timing

A refund is paid within 5 working days of the check.

### RULE-RTN06-0003: Refund vouchers

A customer may take a voucher worth 5% more instead of a refund.
