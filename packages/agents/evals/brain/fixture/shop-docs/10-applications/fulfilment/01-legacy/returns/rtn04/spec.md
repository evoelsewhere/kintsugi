# Articles that arrive broken

How an article that arrives broken is handled.

## Business rules

### RULE-RTN04-0001: Broken window

An article that arrives broken is reported within 7 days of delivery.

### RULE-RTN04-0002: Broken remedy

The customer gets a new one sent at once; there is no refund unless none is left.

### RULE-RTN04-0003: Broken proof

A photo of the article and of its box is attached to the report.
