# Customer cancellation

How a customer cancels a backorder.

## Business rules

### RULE-BKO03-0001: Cancel any time

A customer may cancel a backorder line at any time before it ships.

### RULE-BKO03-0002: Cancel refund

A cancelled paid line is refunded within 5 working days.

### RULE-BKO03-0003: Cancel record

The reason given by the customer is kept for the buyers.
