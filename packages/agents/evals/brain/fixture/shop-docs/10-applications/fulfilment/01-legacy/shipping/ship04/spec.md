# Express delivery

How next-day parcels are sent.

## Business rules

### RULE-SHP04-0001: Express carrier

Express parcels go with the courier Swiftpost.

### RULE-SHP04-0002: Express cut-off

Express is offered for orders placed before 12:00.

### RULE-SHP04-0003: Express regions

Express is not offered on the islands.
