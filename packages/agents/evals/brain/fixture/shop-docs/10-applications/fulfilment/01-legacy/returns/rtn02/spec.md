# Opened articles

How an opened article is taken back.

## Business rules

### RULE-RTN02-0001: Opened window

An opened article may be sent back within 14 days of delivery.

### RULE-RTN02-0002: Opened refund

The price minus a restocking charge of 10% is refunded.

### RULE-RTN02-0003: Opened check

An opened article is checked for missing parts before the refund.
