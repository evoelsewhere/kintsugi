# Reminders

How the legacy dunning batch chases an unpaid invoice.

## Business rules

### RULE-DUN01-0001: Late-payment charge

An invoice still unpaid 30 days after its due date is charged 1.5% of its amount per month, never more than 50.00 in all.

### RULE-DUN01-0002: Reminder letters

A first letter is sent 15 days after the due date and a second one 45 days after it; there is no third letter.
